Scope: the front page (`app/home.jsx`, the `.home` block in `app/styles.css`).
Visitor mode: Operate — success is leaving quickly for something to play, not
lingering here. Audience: the owner on a phone, away from the desk, several
times a day; secondarily whoever he sends a link to.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The front page is a door, not a menu. One record is printed large
enough to be worth crossing the room for; the other four stand as a stub index
carrying no artwork at all. It refuses the category default — five equal cards
in an even grid — because a menu of five is how you end up playing nothing.
The lead is always one of the day's three unrated records, so the position
states something true ("the one you have not heard") instead of implying a
ranking the selection does not make.

OWN-WORLD: The committed world in DESIGN.md, unchanged: bone on warm ink, a
single Sleeve Red carrying state and identity, condensed display caps for
names, a serif for anything read, tracked mono caps for every figure. Square
corners, hairline rules between peers, 2px bone rules closing regions, no
shadow. Recognizable with all content removed by the rule weights alone.

STORY: He opens it, sees what today holds in one glance, and leaves — into the
lead record, into the Shelf, or into Roll. He believes the collection is alive
and being worked through because the page says so in figures, not adjectives.

FIRST VIEWPORT (phone, portrait): a compressed folio rule across the top —
title, today's date in Sleeve Red mono caps, and the running count — at roughly
8% of the viewport. Then the lead: a full-width square sleeve, artist in
tracked mono caps above the album at headline scale, year and genres on one
mono line beneath. A hairline, then the four stubs as mono rows of artist and
title with the year in the left column where a list number would sit, ~65px
each, no artwork. A 2px bone rule closes the set;
the two routes follow as full-width text blocks. The primary action is the lead
sleeve itself, the largest tap target on the page.

FORM: "One Lead, Four in the Margin" — fourth on the ordered list of seven
grounded structures, dealt by the roll and locked by the user over the dealt
lead ("The Running Order"). Seed key cb7a748f.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

The lead sleeve arrives already visible and settles once; the four stubs do not
animate at all. A door opened several times a day must not make anyone watch it
assemble.
